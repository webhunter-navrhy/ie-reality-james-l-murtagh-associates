About the property

Description property

James L. Murtagh Auctioneers are delighted to offer for sale by Public Auction this c. 37 Acre Residential Holding at Baltrasna, Mullingar, Co. Westmeath, N91 AK18.

This superbly located residential farm is situated in a highly sought-after location close to Mullingar town and a wide range of amenities. The holding is laid out in several divisions and benefits from exceptional frontage onto the Royal Canal.

The property includes a single-storey residence extending to approximately 879 sq. ft. (82 sq. m.), with accommodation comprising two bedrooms, kitchen, utility, living room and bathroom. While the residence is in need of renovation, it offers an excellent opportunity to refurbish and extend the property to suit individual requirements. The property may also qualify for the Vacant Property Refurbishment Grant and other available energy upgrade grants, subject to the relevant eligibility criteria.

Accommodation as follows;

Foyer 1.59m x 0.99m

Kitchen 3.51m x 4.34m

Living room 3.45m x 4.34m

Bedroom 1 3.47m x 4.34m

Bedroom 2 3m x 3.05m

Utility 1.81m x 3.05m

Bathroom 3.33m x 1.84m

The yard comprises a three-bay round-roof hay shed with lean-to, together with a range of traditional stone outbuildings, providing useful storage and offering potential for further renovation or adaptation, subject to the necessary consents.

The lands are laid out in several divisions, with approximately half of the holding comprising good-quality lands currently in grass. The remaining lands are considered suitable for native forestry planting, with species appropriate to heavier land types.

Overall, this is a property with considerable potential, combining a residential element, agricultural lands, useful outbuildings and an attractive location close to Mullingar town.
